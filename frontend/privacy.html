Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding your resume, portfolio, and identity data. Learn how we handle your information with strict security.

Last Updated: March 2026
1. Info We Collect 2. How We Use Info 3. Security & Safeguards 4. Your Data Rights 5. Third-Party Services 6. Storage & Cookies 7. Children's Privacy 8. Contact Information

1. Information We Collect

We gather necessary information to deliver our resume builder, live preview, and portfolio publishing features:

  • User Account Details: Name, verified email address, and encrypted credentials. Password hashes are generated using bcrypt (12 rounds) and are never stored in readable plain text.
  • Resume & Career Information: Job titles, work history, institutional degrees, project repositories, technical skills, phone numbers, and professional summaries entered into the builder.
  • Uploaded Resumes (AI OCR): Files uploaded to the AI OCR parser (PDFs, PNGs, JPEGs) are processed client-side via in-browser machine vision engines to extract text directly into your editable resume fields.
  • Technical Metadata: Browser type, timestamps, and session identifiers managed via JSON Web Tokens (JWT) for secure authentication.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is processed exclusively to deliver the functionality of PortfolioAI:

  • Generating and formatting dynamic web portfolios and ATS-optimized PDF downloads.
  • Authenticating your session and ensuring multi-user data isolation.
  • Enabling profile customizations such as avatar uploads and custom theme color palletes.
  • Answering inquiries sent through our Contact Support system via EmailJS.
🛡️ Our Commitment: We NEVER sell, rent, or trade your personal career records or contact information to recruiters, advertisers, or third-party brokers.

3. Data Storage & Security Standards

We apply robust administrative and technical protections:

  • Transport Layer Security: All data transmitted between your browser and our server is secured with high-grade SSL/TLS encryption.
  • Cloud Database Security: Data is persisted in MongoDB Atlas with automated at-rest encryption and strict IP access controls.
  • Stateless Authentication: We utilize tamper-proof JWT authentication with token expiration to guard against unauthorized session reuse.

4. Your Data Rights & Control

In alignment with modern privacy standards (including GDPR and CCPA principles), you retain full rights over your data:

  • Right to Access & Edit: You can view and modify all sections of your resume and profile details at any time from your Dashboard and Editor.
  • Right to Export: Export your resume as an ATS-compliant PDF free of charge at any moment.
  • Right to Permanent Erasure: You can instantly wipe your entire portfolio and resume records using the Reset Portfolio feature on your dashboard.

5. Third-Party Infrastructure Providers

We interface only with reputable service providers bound by strict confidentiality:

  • MongoDB Atlas: Managed database cluster hosting.
  • EmailJS: Client-side contact routing for support messages.
  • CDN Providers: Trusted CDNs (cdnjs, jsdelivr) delivering open-source libraries (SortableJS, html2pdf.js).

6. Cookies & Local Storage

PortfolioAI uses browser localStorage solely for operational purposes: keeping you securely logged in (storing your JWT token) and persisting your preferred color theme (dark/light mode). We do not deploy tracking cookies or behavioral advertising pixels.

7. Children's Privacy

Our platform is intended for professional and educational users aged 13 and above. We do not intentionally collect or maintain personal data from individuals under 13 years of age.
